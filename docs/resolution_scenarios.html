Resolution scenarios

How aspect_ratio resolves to the final video dimensions across T2V, I2V, auto, and explicit-override paths. Each scenario shows the request body, the path through the pipeline, and the dimensions the caller receives back.

input image generation size (/128 grid) cropped away output (post-crop, what the MP4 contains)

The two buckets

Every video the service produces is in exactly one of two sizes:

aspect_ratioInternal gen size /128 gridOutput (what you get)Crop
"16:9" 1920 × 1152 1920 × 1080 36 px off top + 36 px off bottom
"9:16" 1152 × 1920 1080 × 1920 36 px off left + 36 px off right
"auto" Resolves to "16:9" or "9:16" from the input-image orientation (W ≥ H → landscape). T2V with no image falls back to "16:9".

The pipeline path

From request to MP4. Dimensions shown are for the landscape ("16:9") case; portrait swaps width and height.

The crop, in detail

The 3-stage cascade can only emit dimensions on a /128 grid (Stage 1 runs at final/4 and must itself be VAE-/32 aligned). The smallest /128 size that contains 1080 is 1152, so the pipeline generates at 1152 on the short axis and slices the symmetric 36-pixel margin off in pixel space, after VAE decode.

Landscape — 1920×1152 → 1920×1080

Portrait — 1152×1920 → 1080×1920

Why this is loss-free. The crop is a plain tensor slice on the decoded (N, H, W, 3) IMAGE — no resampling, no VAE round-trip. See _center_crop_frames in src/pipeline/triple_stages_comfyui_graph.py.

Why not 1920×1080 directly?

LTX-2.3 itself accepts 1920×1080. 1080 is divisible by 32 (the VAE spatial factor), and Lightricks' one-stage and HDR pipelines both list it as a supported size. The reason our service can't emit 1920×1080 directly is one level up: this repo's ComfyUI workflow (scripts/workflow_3mljpp.py) places Stage 1 at final/4, which means the final dims must be a multiple of 4 × 32 = 128. The HQ two-stage preset Lightricks ships uses Stage 1 at final/2 and so reaches its canonical 1920×1088 on the /64 grid — /128 is not an LTX-2.3 limit, it's a property of this specific workflow's Stage-1 downscale.

The closest /128 options:

This is a workflow choice, not an LTX-2.3 limit. A different ComfyUI workflow that places Stage 1 at final/2 (matching Lightricks' own two-stage shape) would only need /64 and could hit 1920×1088 natively — no crop. We stay on the current final/4 workflow because it's the cascade we tuned + tested against.

Cost of choosing 1152 over 1024 on the current workflow: ~12.5% more pixels at the long-frame Stage 3 and VAE-decode steps — paid once per request to honor the "true 1080p" promise in the public API.
